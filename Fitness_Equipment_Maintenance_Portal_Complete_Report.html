Task 2: Problem Definition and Scope Verified

Deliverables: Problem statement, stakeholder list, objectives, constraints and approved MVP scope.

Problem Statement: Modern commercial fitness centers operate dozens of electro-mechanical machines (treadmills, cable stacks, ellipticals). Equipment downtime currently averages 9.2 days due to paper logbooks, verbal reporting, and missing spare parts. This results in direct member cancellations, physical liability risks, and uncontrolled repair expenditures.

Stakeholder Matrix

Stakeholder Persona Key Responsibilities Pain Points Addressed
Gym Members / Visitors Report broken machines quickly via portal/kiosk. Eliminates interrupted workouts and unaddressed safety hazards.
Gym Floor Staff / Trainers Log incidents with defect description, severity, zone. Replaces lost verbal complaints with instant digital ticket tracking.
Maintenance Technicians Update status (In Repair, Awaiting Parts, Resolved), log costs. Provides clear machine history, fault symptoms, and serial numbers.
Facility Managers / Admins Review KPI dashboard, monitor MTTR, approve budgets. Replaces guesswork with real-time operational uptime metrics.
DevOps / IT Engineers Automate CI/CD, enforce quality gates, manage Puppet IaC. Eliminates configuration drift and deployment downtime.

Objectives & Measurable Success Criteria (SMART)

• Intake Speed: Ticket creation time < 30 seconds via 1-click fault presets.
• MTTR Reduction: Equipment downtime reduced from 9.2 days to < 48 hours.
• Data Completeness: 100% of records capture equipment ID, zone, priority, technician, and cost.
• System Reliability: 99.9% portal uptime with zero configuration drift through idempotent Puppet manifests.

Project Constraints

• Technical: Compatible with Java 17/21/26, Maven 3.9, Docker, and Tomcat/Nginx deployment targets.
• Operational: Intuitive zero-training interface for non-technical gym personnel.
• Security & RBAC: Strict role-based transitions—only Technicians and Admins can resolve or close tickets.
